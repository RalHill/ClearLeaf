# Memorandum of Understanding

**Between:**

**CLEARLEAF INC.**  
("**ClearLeaf**")

**— and —**

**CANADIAN PROFESSIONAL HUMAN RESOURCES ASSOCIATION (CPHR)**  
("**CPHR**")

---

## 1. Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") establishes a non-binding framework for potential collaboration between ClearLeaf and CPHR to:

1. **Provide HR intelligence resources** to CPHR members
2. **Promote employment law education** through ClearLeaf's platform
3. **Integrate CPHR content** into ClearLeaf's news feed and resources
4. **Develop member benefits** that add value for both organizations
5. **Support CPHR's mission** of advancing HR professionalism in Canada

---

## 2. Term & Effective Date

- **Effective Date**: Upon mutual signature (March 2026)
- **Initial Term**: 12 months
- **Renewal**: By mutual written consent
- **Termination**: Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice

---

## 3. ClearLeaf Commitments

### 3.1 CPHR Content Integration
ClearLeaf agrees to:
- Display CPHR-published employment law content in the news feed with proper attribution
- Include a "Source: CPHR Canada" byline on all CPHR content
- Provide attribution links back to CPHR's original content (when published online)
- Update or remove content if CPHR requests corrections within 7 days of publication

### 3.2 Member Visibility
ClearLeaf agrees to:
- Feature CPHR as a trusted knowledge partner on the "About" page
- Include CPHR in the "News Sources" section of the platform
- Display CPHR member badge eligibility in the pricing/benefits sections
- Respond to CPHR inquiries about platform usage within 5 business days

### 3.3 Technical Requirements
ClearLeaf commits to:
- Maintain RSS feed integration for CPHR content (if applicable)
- Ensure CPHR content is tagged with "CPHR" topic for filtering
- Provide monthly reports on CPHR content engagement (if requested)
- Notify CPHR of planned downtime affecting content feed (48 hours' notice)

---

## 4. CPHR Commitments

### 4.1 Content Authorization
CPHR agrees to:
- Grant ClearLeaf permission to republish CPHR content in the news feed
- Ensure content is legally cleared for republication (no copyright conflicts)
- Provide content in a machine-readable format (RSS, JSON, or PDF)
- Update ClearLeaf if RSS feed URLs or content distribution methods change

### 4.2 Member Communication
CPHR agrees to:
- Promote ClearLeaf platform to its membership (including member discount codes, if applicable)
- Include ClearLeaf as a recommended resource in CPHR publications or webinars
- Provide a quote or endorsement for use in ClearLeaf marketing (optional)
- Share feedback on member experience with ClearLeaf

### 4.3 Partnership Representation
CPHR agrees to:
- Acknowledge ClearLeaf as a knowledge partner in relevant communications
- Refer inquiries about employment law intelligence to ClearLeaf where appropriate
- Support ClearLeaf's marketing efforts (social media mentions, newsletter mentions)

---

## 5. Member Benefits (Optional)

The parties may establish special terms for CPHR members, including:
- **Discount Code**: % off annual Professional or Team subscriptions
- **Exclusive Features**: Early access to new provinces or features
- **CE Credits**: Potential integration with CPHR Professional Development Program
- **Bulk Licensing**: Volume pricing for CPHR chapters or study groups

Details of member benefits will be outlined in a separate Statement of Work (SOW), if agreed upon.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

### 6.1 Content Ownership
- **CPHR Content**: Remains the exclusive property of CPHR
- **ClearLeaf Platform**: Remains the exclusive property of ClearLeaf
- **Joint Materials**: Developed collaboratively with mutual consent ownership

### 6.2 Attribution Requirements
All reproduced content must include clear attribution:
- "Content from CPHR Canada" or "By [CPHR Author Name]"
- Link to original source (if online)
- Date of original publication

### 6.3 Derivative Works
Neither party may create derivative works (summaries, translations, or modifications) of the other's content without written permission.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

### 7.1 Confidential Information
Information marked "CONFIDENTIAL" or reasonably understood to be confidential may not be disclosed to third parties without consent.

### 7.2 Exceptions
Confidentiality does not apply to information that is:
- Already publicly available
- Independently developed
- Legally required to disclose
- Required for compliance with law

---

## 8. Data & Privacy

### 8.1 Member Data
- ClearLeaf will NOT share member usage data with CPHR without consent
- CPHR will NOT use ClearLeaf data for competing platforms
- Both parties comply with PIPEDA and applicable privacy laws

### 8.2 Analytics Reporting
ClearLeaf may provide CPHR with anonymized, aggregated statistics about:
- Total number of CPHR content views
- Popular CPHR topics among users
- Overall user engagement metrics

No individual user data will be shared.

---

## 9. Disclaimers

### 9.1 No Endorsement of Legal Advice
- CPHR does not endorse ClearLeaf as a substitute for legal counsel
- ClearLeaf remains clearly identified as informational, not legal advice
- Both parties maintain independent liability for their content

### 9.2 Content Accuracy
- ClearLeaf is responsible for the accuracy of knowledge base content
- CPHR is responsible for the accuracy of CPHR-published content
- Each party indemnifies the other for inaccuracies in their respective content

### 9.3 No Employment Relationship
This MOU does not create an employment or exclusive relationship. Both parties remain independent and may pursue other partnerships.

---

## 10. Term Conditions & Performance

### 10.1 Good Faith
Both parties agree to perform under this MOU in good faith and cooperate on implementing the provisions.

### 10.2 Change Management
If either party wishes to modify terms, they must notify the other in writing with 60 days' notice for material changes.

### 10.3 Performance Standards
- Content feed must be updated within 24 hours of new CPHR publication
- Bug reports or issues must be addressed within 10 business days
- Quarterly check-in calls to assess partnership performance

---

## 11. Termination

### 11.1 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this MOU by providing 30 days' written notice.

### 11.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately if:
- The other party materially breaches this MOU and fails to cure within 15 days
- The other party engages in unlawful or unethical conduct
- The other party infringes intellectual property rights

### 11.3 Effects of Termination
Upon termination:
- ClearLeaf must remove all CPHR-specific branding within 30 days
- CPHR content already published may remain on ClearLeaf (with attribution)
- Member discounts (if applicable) expire
- Both parties' obligations under Sections 6, 7, 8 continue indefinitely

---

## 12. Limitation of Liability

### 12.1 No Warranties
This MOU is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind. Neither party guarantees:
- Uninterrupted service
- Error-free content
- Specific business results
- Third-party compliance

### 12.2 Liability Cap
Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages. Each party's liability is capped at the direct costs of remedying the breach.

---

## 13. Dispute Resolution

### 13.1 Good Faith Discussion
If a dispute arises, the parties agree to first attempt resolution through good faith discussion between senior representatives.

### 13.2 Escalation
If unresolved after 30 days, either party may pursue mediation or arbitration under Canadian law.

### 13.3 Governing Law
This MOU is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Ontario, Canada.

---

## 14. Entire Agreement

This MOU, together with any separate SOW or exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior understandings. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
